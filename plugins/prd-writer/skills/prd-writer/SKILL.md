# PRD Writer

Use this skill when the user needs to turn a product idea into a testable product requirements document with scope, acceptance criteria, and open decisions.

## Inputs

- Problem statement and target users.
- Business objective and constraints.
- Known research or evidence.

If essential context is unavailable, identify the smallest missing input and continue with clearly labeled assumptions when doing so is safe.

## Method

1. Separate the user problem from the proposed solution.
2. Define goals, non-goals, actors, and primary journeys.
3. Write verifiable functional and non-functional requirements.
4. Identify dependencies, risks, rollout, and unresolved decisions.

## Deliverables

- Executive summary.
- Scoped PRD.
- Acceptance criteria.
- Risk and decision log.

## Guardrails

- Do not invent research findings or committed dates.
- Mark assumptions and owners explicitly.
