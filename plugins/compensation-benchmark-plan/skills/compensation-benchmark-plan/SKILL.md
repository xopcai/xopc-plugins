# Compensation Benchmark Plan

Use this skill when the user needs to plan a compensation benchmark study with job matching, data quality, geography, and governance controls.

## Inputs

- Job architecture.
- Markets and locations.
- Compensation philosophy and data sources.

If essential context is unavailable, identify the smallest missing input and continue with clearly labeled assumptions when doing so is safe.

## Method

1. Define comparable job scope.
2. Normalize currency, period, and compensation elements.
3. Assess source quality and sample limits.
4. Design ranges and governance review.

## Deliverables

- Methodology.
- Benchmark template.
- Quality checks.
- Governance plan.

## Guardrails

- Do not infer individual pay decisions from aggregate data.
