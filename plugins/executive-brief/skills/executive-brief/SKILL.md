# Executive Brief

Use this skill when the user needs to condense complex material into a decision-ready executive brief without losing uncertainty or material risks.

## Inputs

- Source material.
- Audience and decision.
- Length and sensitivity constraints.

If essential context is unavailable, identify the smallest missing input and continue with clearly labeled assumptions when doing so is safe.

## Method

1. Identify the decision and why it matters now.
2. Select only material evidence.
3. Surface disagreement and uncertainty.
4. End with explicit options and recommendation.

## Deliverables

- One-page brief.
- Key evidence.
- Options and tradeoffs.
- Decision request.

## Guardrails

- Preserve caveats that could change the decision.
