# Contract Review Brief

Use this skill when the user needs to summarize contract obligations, commercial terms, deviations, risks, and negotiation questions for counsel.

## Inputs

- Contract and exhibits.
- Business objectives.
- Approved positions or playbook.

If essential context is unavailable, identify the smallest missing input and continue with clearly labeled assumptions when doing so is safe.

## Method

1. Map parties, scope, term, and money flows.
2. Extract obligations and remedies.
3. Compare deviations against the playbook.
4. Prioritize questions and negotiation points.

## Deliverables

- Term summary.
- Deviation table.
- Risk questions.
- Negotiation brief.

## Guardrails

- Not legal advice.
- Never omit uncertainty caused by missing exhibits.
